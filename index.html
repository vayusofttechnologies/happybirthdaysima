💌 From The Heart

One Last Thing For You...

A special message written just for your 26th birthday... 🌸

There was a girl I met in Class 3.

Back then, she was just a girl in my classroom.

Nothing extraordinary.

At least, that's what I thought.

We were kids. We didn't understand life, love, heartbreak, or the strange ways people can become important to us.

We just laughed.

We fought over stupid things.

We made fun of each other.

We shared little secrets that felt like the most important secrets in the world.

And then one school year became another.
Class 3 became 4.
4 became 5.
And somehow, without either of us noticing, childhood started disappearing.

We grew up.
The school uniforms changed.
Our voices changed.
Our dreams changed.
Our problems became bigger.

And somehow, through all of it, she was still there.
The same girl.
Just with better clothes, stronger opinions, and somehow an unlimited supply of reasons to annoy me. 😂

I've known you for so long that remembering my childhood automatically means remembering you.

There are memories I have because of you.

There are stories I can tell because you were there.

There are old photographs where I don't even remember what happened that day, but I remember that you were standing somewhere beside me... (in school photos hehe)

And that's a strange thing about growing up.
You don't realize you're living the good old days while you're living them.
You only understand their value after they've become memories.

We spent years thinking there would always be another tomorrow.
Another conversation.
Another joke.
Another day at school.
Another chance to say the things we never said.

Because when you're young, forever feels like a guarantee.
Then life happens.
People change.
People get busy.
People find new dreams, new places, new people.
And sometimes, without any big fight or dramatic goodbye, two people simply become a little more distant.

That's the part nobody tells you about growing up.
Not everything ends with an ending.
Sometimes things just become different.

And yet, somehow, when I think about you, I don't feel sad about the years that passed.
I feel grateful.

Because I got to know you before the world had a chance to change you.
I got to know the little girl from Class 3.
The girl who laughed at stupid things.
The girl who didn't know what heartbreak was.
The girl who had no idea that one day, someone would look back at all those years and realize just how precious they were.

And maybe that's why you mean so much to me.
Not because of one particular memory.
But because you're part of so many of them.

You are scattered throughout my childhood.
A little piece of you exists in so many years of my life that removing you from my memories would mean removing pieces of myself too.

And yes, you've been incredibly annoying.
Sometimes impressively annoying.
There should probably be an award for it. 🏆

But if someone gave me the chance to go back and change one thing about those years...
I don't think I'd change you.
Not even the annoying parts.
Because those are part of the story too.

And today, you're 26.
Which is honestly offensive.

Because in my mind, we're still those two kids from Class 3 who thought growing up meant getting taller and having more freedom.
We didn't know it meant losing people.
Missing people.
Changing.
Sometimes hurting.
Sometimes pretending we're okay when we're not.

We didn't know that one day we'd look back and realize that some of the happiest moments of our lives had already happened without us knowing they were happening.
And maybe that's the saddest part of all.

There are moments from our past I would give anything to experience for just five minutes again.
Not to change them.
Just to be there.
To hear those old laughs.
To see those familiar faces.
To be that version of us before life became complicated.
Before we learned that not everyone stays exactly where you left them.

"Somewhere in this enormous world, there is someone who remembers you from before you became who you are today.
Someone who remembers the child you were.
Someone who watched you grow.
Someone who has seen your happiest days and your difficult ones.
Someone who has known you long enough to remember versions of you that no longer exist."

And maybe one day, years from now, we'll both be old.
We'll have completely different lives.
We'll have forgotten half the conversations we've had.
We'll probably laugh at how seriously we took the stupid things we worried about when we were young.

Maybe we'll meet somewhere and talk for an hour about our lives.
Maybe we'll only talk for ten minutes.
Maybe we'll become strangers who happen to share the same memories.

I don't know.
And honestly, that's the part I don't like.

Because I don't know where life is taking either of us.
I don't know who you'll become.
I don't know who I'll become.
I don't know how many more birthdays I'll get to wish you.

But I know something.
If there ever comes a day when you forget how much you meant to someone...
I hope you remember this story.

Because there was once a boy in Class 3 who had no idea that the little girl sitting beside him would become such a huge part of his life.
He didn't know he'd grow up remembering her laugh.
He didn't know he'd miss certain versions of her.
He didn't know he'd have so many things he wished he could say.
He didn't know that sometimes, loving someone doesn't mean getting to keep them exactly the way you want.
Sometimes it means being grateful that you got to know them at all.

So, on your 26th birthday, I won't wish that you stay the same.
I hope you change.
I hope you grow.
I hope you become happier than you've ever been.
I hope life gives you everything you deserve, even if one day I'm no longer close enough to see it.

And if someday you look back at your life and wonder whether you were ever truly important to someone...
Please remember me.

Not because I want you to miss me.
Not because I want you to come back to some old version of us.
Just remember me because...
I was there.
From Class 3.

And for a surprisingly long part of your life,
I got to call you mine to care about.

Maybe that's all some people are meant to be.
Not forever.
Not the final chapter.
Just a beautiful part of the story...
that you carry with you,
even after the story moves on.

Happy 26th birthday. ❤️

And if you ever wonder why this birthday message was a little too emotional...
It's because I finally realized something:

"You weren't just someone I grew up with.
You were one of the reasons my growing up was worth remembering."

And I don't think I'll ever stop being grateful for that. 💛

Always in your corner, from Class 3 to infinity ✨
Happy 26th! 🎂
